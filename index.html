The basics

What is Still Water?

A calm koi pond for a quiet minute. Watch the koi, listen to the water, and make a wish. There is nothing to win and no rush.

How to play
  1. Tap the water to make ripples. Nearby koi may dart away.
  2. Tap Throw coin, then tap the water to make a wish. It costs 1 coin.
  3. Tap Feed koi, then tap the water to drop food for the koi.
  4. Tap Shop to add koi and pond extras.
  5. Tap the … button at the bottom right for settings. Tap Done when you finish throwing or feeding.
Game modes

Version 1 — Relax: a gentle pond. Koi stay the same size.

Version 2 — Normal: koi grow when you feed them, and the shop gets a Progress tab.

Switch in … → Game → Game Mode Version. Your coins and koi are kept when you switch.

How do koi grow? (Version 2)

Each koi grows by eating food. Every piece it eats counts for that koi.

SizeFood eaten
Small0–59
Medium60–179
Large180–359
Extra Large360 or more
  • Each tap of Feed costs 1 coin and drops 10 pieces of food.
  • Small koi dart faster and notice food from farther away.
  • A message appears when a koi levels up.
  • A koi that reaches Extra Large earns +10 coins, once per koi, and gets a gold shimmer.
  • See every koi's progress in the Progress tab.

Coins and koi

Coins
  • You start with 20 coins.
  • Throwing a coin or tapping Feed costs 1 coin each.
  • Koi and pond extras are bought in the Spend coins tab. Kohaku and Tancho are free. Ogon is 40, Showa 60, Kujaku 80, Chagoi 50. Lotus flowers are 25, Soft rain 40, Evening 60.
  • In Version 2, an Extra Large koi gives +10 coins once.
  • Buy coins is a demo. No real money is charged and the coins are added straight away.
Koi and pond extras
  • You can own up to 5 of each koi variety.
  • Tap − to hide a koi. You keep it. Tap + to bring it back for free.
  • Extras you own can be switched on and off in the shop or in … → Visuals.

Sound and time

Timer and sounds
  • The timer at the top counts up the time you spend at the pond. Tap it to open settings.
  • Pick 10, 15, 30 or 60 mins in the settings to hear a gentle bell when time is up. The timer then counts down. Tap the same length again to clear it.
  • Sound starts after your first tap.
  • Each sound has a slider and a switch. Mute all silences everything.

Your device

Install on your phone
  • Android: tap ⋮ in your browser, then Install app.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • After your first visit it also works offline.
Is my progress saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device. Clearing your browser data resets it. It is not synced across devices. To tidy up, open Settings, then Pond care, and choose Clean pond (removes coins from the pond floor) or Reset progress (every koi goes back to Small).
